OK - so what do we do about it?
Don't shut it down - sell it

Shutting it down throws away assets a shutdown can't recover. You have real things a buyer would pay for that a wind-down just writes off:

What's actually on the table
• An active customer base still buying your other product lines - not just this unit's book
• Purpose-built tooling and fulfillment workflows, already running
• Brand and domain assets specific to the category
• Existing supplier agreements, transferable to a buyer

Run a structured sale process instead of a wind-down. A declining category doesn't mean these assets are worthless to someone else - it means they're worth more to a buyer than they are to you carrying the loss.
