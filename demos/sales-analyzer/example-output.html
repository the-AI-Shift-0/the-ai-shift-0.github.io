Section 4
Call Analysis Framework

The Five Scoring Dimensions (score each 1–10 with evidence, then weight and combine)

Rapport – 25%

Did the buyer decide that being candid with this rep was safe?

  • 8–10: Volunteered material information more than once, unprompted, including something awkward or political
  • 5–7: Answered fully and added some context, but stayed inside the topics the rep raised
  • 1–4: Correct, short, factual answers and no volunteered information

Objection Handling – 30%

Did the rep treat the objection as information, or as an attack?

  • 8–10: Played it back in the buyer's own words, isolated it, answered with evidence, then checked the answer landed
  • 5–7: Answered the surface sentence competently but never found the mechanism underneath it
  • 1–4: Rebutted instantly, or agreed and moved on with nothing resolved

Clarity – 20%

Could the buyer restate the offer to a colleague after this call?

  • 8–10: The buyer said the offer back in their own words, correctly
  • 5–7: Explained clearly, but never tested against the buyer's understanding
  • 1–4: A feature list, jargon, or three different descriptions of the same thing

Needs Matching – 15%

Is every benefit traceable to a need the buyer stated in this transcript?

  • 8–10: Every claim tied back to something the buyer said out loud
  • 5–7: Mostly relevant, with some untethered capability talk
  • 1–4: The standard pitch, delivered regardless of what was uncovered

Authority and Frame – 10%

Did the rep own the process without pressuring the decision?

  • 8–10: Set the structure, stated a real constraint plainly, left the decision entirely with the buyer
  • 5–7: Led most of the call but ceded the process on price or timeline
  • 1–4: Followed the buyer, or manufactured a deadline that was not real

Section 5
Key Moments to Flag
  • 📊 Talk ratio: if rep spoke more than 60% of the call, flag it
  • ⚡ Engagement shift: the exact moment energy changed (positive or negative) - quote the line
  • 🛑 First objection: how it arrived, how it was handled, whether it was resolved or bypassed
  • 💰 Price / budget objection: specifically watch for this - it kills the most deals. Note exactly when and how it came up.
  • 🤝 Closing attempt: was there a specific ask for next steps? Were they concrete and owned?

Industry-Specific Patterns (Online Coaching / Education)

  • "I've tried programs before and they didn't work" - this is a trust objection, not a price objection. Probe for what specifically failed.
  • Watch for: reps pitching the program before establishing belief that the prospect can succeed.
  • "Price" in coaching usually means "I don't believe the ROI" - push on what the cost of NOT solving this is, not on payment plans.
