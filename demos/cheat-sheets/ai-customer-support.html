AI Customer Support

Recording cheat sheet. Demo 1 is a whiteboard, then one real ticket. Demo 2 follows the AI Caller pattern.

Standing recording rules - do these before you hit record

  1. Hide the macOS Dock. System Settings > Desktop & Dock > Automatically hide and show the Dock: ON.
  2. Hide the app sidebar. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all collapse theirs - collapse it before the first frame, not mid-take.
  3. Hide the browser bookmarks bar. Cmd+Shift+B.
  4. Hide the URL / address bar wherever possible. Full screen, or a distraction-free window. Where the URL has to be visible because it is the point, show only that one URL and nothing around it.

Raised again on the 2026-08-19 call as a repeat offence. Do it at capture, never in the edit - "the resolution becomes an issue" and it comes back not done.

Demo 1, first half - the whiteboard

Drawn by hand. No screen. Your rule for this one: if it's great, make it stupider.

  1. Ticket comes in.
  2. It logs in and checks the history.
  3. It crafts a reply.
  4. It goes to AI model two.
  5. Model two says yes, good to go - send the reply.
  6. Model two says no - it says what the problem is - it tries again.
  7. Good to go, send. Or no - escalate into Slack.

These are your own steps from the 2026-08-19 call, in your order. You are deriving the same list yourself from Mehak's transcript. Only come back to Apurv if that fails.

  1. Show a ticket that has already come in and been answered.

Live or replay is still unanswered. Apurv said the page will not generate at that time; you said "this is not a live stream, bro, I can wait". Until he confirms, plan for a ticket already handled and treat live as a bonus.

Blur list - this is a real support desk

Blur: customer names, email addresses, order and invoice numbers, company names, phone numbers, anything in the sidebar list of other tickets. Pick the ticket before the slot, not during it.

⛔ Demo 2 is a BLUEPRINT, not working software

Your own words on why:

There's nothing you can do in terms of "upload this thing to a project and then it will reply to your customer emails". Just, no. It is a full thing.

So what they get is a blueprint of what their setup should look like - the pieces it needs, how a ticket moves through them, and the checks that stop a bad reply going out. Something they build, or hand to a developer.

Never say or imply: they get a system that answers their emails; it works out of the box; upload this and you're done; we hand you the pipeline.

This exact defect shipped before. Copy implying the lead receives working software went out across 28 places in four repos on 2026-08-11 for AI Caller, and it was caught after the fact. It is bigger here, because this thing is a helpdesk, a CRM, two models and a Slack escalation. Say it wrong on camera and it cannot be fixed in the edit.

Never saySay instead

evaluator node, the second model scores the draft "a completely different AI reads it and marks it - right, wrong, or a human needs to see this"

The viewer is an owner with a support team, not a builder. Naming the plumbing tells them this is not for them. Describe what it does.

OPEN QUESTIONS - answer before filming
