AI Customer Support

Demo 1: whiteboard, then one real ticket. Demo 2: the live opt-in page.

Before you hit record

  • Dock hidden - auto-hide ON.
  • App sidebar collapsed.
  • Bookmarks bar off - Cmd+Shift+B.
  • URL bar hidden. If a URL is the point, show that one alone.

At capture, not in the edit.

Demo 1a - the whiteboard

Hand-drawn, no screen. Rule: if it's great, make it stupider.

  1. Ticket comes in.
  2. It logs in and checks the history.
  3. It crafts a reply.
  4. It goes to AI model two.
  5. Model two says yes, good to go - send the reply.
  6. Model two says no - it says what the problem is - it tries again.
  7. Good to go, send. Or no - escalate into Slack.
  • Model two marks the reply against the knowledge base and the escalation rules: correct, incorrect, or escalation needed. A refund request is always escalated, never auto-answered.

Demo 1b - one real ticket

  • Show a ticket already answered.
  • Show the drafted reply.
  • Approval is backend - narrate it, do not show it.
  • Blur: names, emails, order and invoice numbers, companies, phone numbers, the sidebar ticket list.
Tickets come in from Jack. You just see the AI has replied. What it did: went into the CRM, checked the history and the purchases, wrote this reply, ran it past a second model.

⛔ Demo 2 is a BLUEPRINT, not working software

  • No "upload this and it replies to your emails" version exists. It is a full system.
  • They get a blueprint: the pieces, how a ticket moves, the checks that stop a bad reply.
  • Never imply: it answers their emails, works out of the box, done on upload.

This shipped wrong on AI Caller. On camera it cannot be edited out.

  6. Which One? * - conditional
  7. Tell Us Which CRM * - conditional

Never say on camera

Never saySay instead
vector store, embeddings, retrieval, RAG "it looks up just the part of our knowledge that answers this"
evaluator node, the second model scores the draft "a different AI marks it - right, wrong, or a human needs to see this"
webhook, API, pipeline, workflow automation, tool brand names "it plugs into the helpdesk the team already uses"
it hallucinates "it makes things up - the second check catches that"

The viewer is an owner, not a builder.
