Claude Code Adoption

Demo 1 = trophy case of in-house builds. Demo 2 = The Hackathon Playbook opt-in.

Before you hit record

  • Hide four things, at capture, never in post: the Dock, the app sidebar, the bookmarks bar, the URL bar.
  • If a URL must be on screen, show that one and nothing around it.

All four have leaked something into a customer-facing frame before.

Two rules over the whole video

  • Nobody in Movement 2 is named or shown. Ceiling is relationship plus age: "my eight-year-old son". No face, no name, no school.
  • Demo beats script. Change the spoken line, never the product. Fixes are in SCRIPT WORDING below - read it before the shoot.

Demo 1 - the trophy case

Montage of builds that already exist. 10-15 sec a slot, 60-90 sec total. Nothing built on camera.

Slot 1 - the wall (~10 sec)

  • Four to six demos flashing past. No narration on individual ones.
Everything you're about to see was built in-house. Not one developer. Not one agency.

The script says "people on my team" - that contradicts the family half.

Slot 2 - the podcast lead finder

  • No asset on disk in either repo. Capture it on the day or drop the slot.
  • Do not read the scripted line. It takes a bio and finds shows for clients, with contact emails - no outreach step.

Slot 3 - three in-between builds

  • Name all three live, in the montage between the podcast tool and the AI caller - what each one is and what it replaced, out loud, including the "this one does..." line. Put a real thing there, never a placeholder word.
  • Sales analyzer and FB audience consolidator are on disk. The funnel connector has no asset on disk - capture or swap it.

Slot 4 - the AI caller

  • Hold beat, not a flash. Let it play: lead in, phone rings, conversation, appointment booked.
Built by someone here who, again, is not a developer.

Slot 5 - Movement 2, descending age

  • Family member (older gen), nephew 13, nephew 10, hard cut to the game mod - gameplay only.
  • Blocked - see OPEN QUESTIONS.
This wasn't built by someone on my team. This was built by my eight-year-old son.

Full beat of silence, then:

So whatever story you're telling yourself about your team not being technical enough - let it go.

On screen: blur, and never say

  • Blur: client and lead names, phone numbers, revenue and contract figures, podcast host names, the test inbox, other tabs and window titles, any real vendor name in the form.
  • Never say: API, webhook, repo, terminal, CLI, deploy, run-of-show.
  • First-name sweep across every frame - a separate pass after the blur.
  • The AI caller video must publish before this one.

Demo 2 - The Hackathon Playbook LIVE, 200 TODAY

https://theaishift.biz/tools/hackathon-plan/master-trove/
  • Slug is hackathon-plan, not the video's name. There is no /tools/claude-code-adoption/ page - never type it on camera.
  • One name everywhere: The Hackathon Playbook - video, page, email, file.

The form - four fields, two steps

  • Heading on the page is The Hackathon Playbook. Not "Master Trove" - unlike the other tools.
  • Step 1: Your Name * · Email Address *, then Next. Step 2, "Your Team": How Many People Are Taking Part? * (a dropdown) · What Do You Currently Pay Other People to Do? *, then Get My Hackathon Playbook. All four required.
  • ⚠ The script walks five. Three do not exist - "what your people actually do", "work your team grudgingly does by hand", "what you'd most want them to build first". Do not narrate them.
  • Type the outsourced answer as a comma-separated list, short labels only. A prose sentence with a comma or "and" makes the file print a refusal line.
Bookkeeping, video editing, cold email outreach, first-line customer support

Shot order

  1. Fill the page as a real owner and submit.
  2. The inbox, email arriving. Blur the test address and all around it.
  3. Open the file, scroll slowly, then hold on the penalty rule and read it aloud. That is the wow.
  4. The starter kit at the end - setup, commands, the "how to get unstuck" prompt.
  • No live generation. The deliverable is the story.
  • The playbook assumes a paid Claude plan - Pro, Max or Teams. If you say it on camera, say it once, plainly: the free plan does not include Claude Code.
  • Generate a fresh copy on the day and read it before filming. Never quote the file from this sheet.

SCRIPT WORDING - your call

1. Do not claim the plan is calibrated

calibrated to your team, your size, and the exact work.
It's the actual plan we ran - same week, same scoring, same rules - with your team size and your own list of outsourced work written into it.

Measured today: two plans from opposite extreme answers came back 94% character-identical. Only two fields reach the generator.

Say it adapts the sizing and the outsourcing section. Never that the whole plan was rebuilt for them.

2. The number of fields you walk

[the script walks five fields, one at a time]
Two questions about your team. That's the whole form. Then it emails you the plan.

Change the line, never the form - shortest form on the site is the selling point.
