AI Mentor

Both demos run live in one ChatGPT window. Install the rules account-wide, then use the pushback prompts at each decision.

Before you hit record

  • Hide four things at capture, never in post: the Dock, the app sidebar, the bookmarks bar, the URL bar.

⛔ Pick the scenario before roll - only you can

Non-business, because the AI has no context for the company on camera. Real money or real stakes, because the ruleset opens with "find what is wrong with my thinking before it costs me money".

  • Buying a rental property you have never managed, sums already "done".
  • Quitting in three months for a side project, on 8 months of savings.
  • A large personal purchase on finance you have already decided on.

Run the chosen one through ChatGPT once before roll, before and after, so the before/after is known rather than hoped for.

Demo 1 - account-wide

Setup

  • Clean account: Custom Instructions empty, memory off. Check, do not assume.
  • Fresh chat, no scrollback. Ruleset PDF open in another app at PART 1.

On camera

  1. Type the scenario. Let it run in full. It agrees.
  2. Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions. Film the navigation.
  3. Paste PART 1 - everything between >>> COPY FROM HERE >>> and <<< STOP COPYING HERE <<<, 1,467 characters. Save on camera.
  4. New chat. Same scenario, word for word.
  5. It pushes back. Hold on it.

If the rules are already in settings, the first answer is not agreeable and there is no demo.

Demo 2 - the prompt pack

PART 3 of the PDF - THE PUSHBACK PROMPTS, nine of them. Nothing is uploaded - you paste from the PDF.

  1. Bring a real decision.
  2. Open PUSHBACK 1. In a lead's copy it arrives already filled in with their own form answers - their decision, their business, the answer they were hoping to hear. Point at that on camera.
  3. Paste it. Let it argue back.
  4. Scroll PART 3. Eight more, for other moments.
You install PART 1 once. Then you come back to PART 3 at every decision. That is the difference between it working in week three and being abandoned in week one.

Demo 1 is the install. Demo 2 is what you actually do afterwards.

Script wording - your call before the read

1. Two banned words you actually speak

... a prompt plus a config file you paste straight into your own AI, so it starts telling you the truth on the very next decision you bring it.
... a set of prompts you paste in at the decision, plus the standing rules underneath them - so it starts pushing back on the very next decision you bring it, and shows you its working.

"System prompt" and "config file" are both banned and both sit in this one sentence. Approve the replacement before the read.

2. The ten-message rule

re-read these rules every ten messages and make sure you're still holding to them. That was the version that finally worked.
I didn't ask it to police itself. It can't. Every ten messages or so, the rules go back in front of it. And the moment it starts agreeing with me again, I start a clean conversation and put the rules back on top. That's the version that finally held.

Drop the "so it can't drift" clause - a model does not police itself on a schedule. Yes or no, before the read. The replacement is a longer read; check it against runtime.

Open questions - answer before filming

  3. The banned-words sentence. Approve the replacement above.
  4. The ten-message rewording. Yes or no.
