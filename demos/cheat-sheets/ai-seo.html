AI SEO

Recording cheat sheet. Demo 1 is two prompts, run live in your own AI. Demo 2 is the built AI Search Kit mockup.

Standing recording rules - do these before you hit record

  1. Hide the macOS Dock. System Settings > Desktop & Dock > Automatically hide and show the Dock: ON.
  2. Hide the app sidebar. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all collapse theirs - collapse it before the first frame, not mid-take.
  3. Hide the browser bookmarks bar. Cmd+Shift+B.
  4. Hide the URL / address bar wherever possible. Full screen, or a distraction-free window. Where the URL has to be visible because it is the point, show only that one URL and nothing around it.

Raised again on the 2026-08-19 call as a repeat offence. Do it at capture, never in the edit - "the resolution becomes an issue" and it comes back not done.

The two prompts - type them exactly, do not fix them

These are the two you were sent. They are on this sheet so nothing has to be remembered on the day.

What do you know about coachfoundation.com?
Who is Sai C.N.G. Blackbyrn?
Why it is worded that way
Prompt 1 names the domain on purpose. Without it the AI stops and asks whether you mean the Coach handbag company's non-profit arm. The domain kills that question before it is asked.
Prompt 2 uses the full name on purpose. Drop the initials and it guesses at who you mean.

Both were tested. Nothing negative comes back for either.

Shortening either one on camera changes the answer you get. Type them as written.

Demo 1 - ask the AI about us, on camera

  1. Clean window, one tab, no other chats in the sidebar.
  2. Prompt 1. Let it answer. Hold on the answer long enough to read.
  3. Prompt 2. Same.
  4. Point at what the answer got right, what it missed, and where it got it from.

Coach Foundation only. Never a client's. Your own words: "So I'll use ours as an example. I won't use other people's."

Run each prompt three or more times before the take. The same question gets a different answer each run. Say that out loud in the video - a viewer who tries it and gets something else concludes we faked it.

Nothing on screen is a measured result

  • No AI SEO audit of Coach Foundation exists. Nobody has run one and recorded it.
  • So: no before-and-after, no percentage, no "we went from X to Y", no citation count.
  • If the honest answer is that the AI barely knows us, that is the material. This audience trusts it more than a win.
  • No timeline. No promise of being cited.
  • No invented example company.

Never say on camera: AEO, GEO, LLMO, answer engine optimisation, grounding, citations, hallucinate, query, system prompt, project knowledge.
