AI SEO

Demo 1: two prompts, live. Demo 2: the AI Search Kit opt-in page and mockup.

Before you hit record

  • Hide the Dock, the app sidebar, the bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B) and the URL bar - at capture, never in post.

The two prompts - type exactly

What do you know about coachfoundation.com?
Who is Sai C.N.G. Blackbyrn?
  • Prompt 1 names the domain so the AI does not ask about Coach the handbag brand.
  • Prompt 2 uses the initials so it does not guess.
  • Both tested, nothing negative. Shortening either changes the answer.

Demo 1 - run it

  1. Clean window, one tab, no other chats in the sidebar.
  2. Prompt 1. Hold on the answer long enough to read.
  3. Prompt 2. Same.
  4. Call out what it got right, what it missed, and its sources.
  • Coach Foundation only. Never a client.
  • Nothing on screen needs blurring - no client appears.
  • Run each prompt 3+ times before the take. Say on camera that answers vary.

A viewer who gets a different answer concludes we faked it.

Nothing on screen is a measured result

  • No AI SEO audit of Coach Foundation has ever been recorded.
  • No before-and-after, percentage, citation count, timeline or invented company.
  • If the AI barely knows us, that is the material.
  • Never say: AEO, GEO, LLMO, answer engine optimisation, grounding, citations, hallucinate, query, system prompt, project knowledge.
