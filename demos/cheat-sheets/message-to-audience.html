Message to Audience

Recording cheat sheet. Demo 1 is the built four-screen walkthrough. Demo 2 is the Audience Messaging Expert Master Trove opt-in.

Standing recording rules - do these before you hit record

  1. Hide the macOS Dock. System Settings > Desktop & Dock > Automatically hide and show the Dock: ON.
  2. Hide the app sidebar. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all collapse theirs - collapse it before the first frame, not mid-take.
  3. Hide the browser bookmarks bar. Cmd+Shift+B.
  4. Hide the URL / address bar wherever possible. Full screen, or a distraction-free window. Where the URL has to be visible because it is the point, show only that one URL and nothing around it.

Why: the frame should show the work, not the machine. Every one of these is a leak path - open tabs, bookmarks, internal URLs, folder names and staff names have all reached a customer-facing frame before. Hiding them costs four seconds and removes the whole class of problem.

Settled decisions - do not reopen these on the day

  • The show is decided: The Amy Porterfield Show, hosted by Amy Porterfield. It is named on screen and nothing is blurred. The show name, the host name and the URL all render sharp. Blurring them was considered and rejected.
  • Demo 2 is copy-paste, not a link. Demo 1 takes a link. Demo 2 takes a paste. These are different mechanics and the script must not blur them together. The reason has changed and the reason matters - this was settled on 2026-08-11 on the basis that the assistant could not fetch anything at all, and that is no longer true. See SCRIPT WORDING below before you say anything on camera about what the AI can and cannot reach. The paste stays: it is the one path that works for every source. The old justification for it does not.

Banned outright: any download, listener or audience-size figure. No such number exists publicly for this show. It must not go on screen, it must not be spoken, and it must not be estimated out loud. If a number for audience size appears anywhere in a cut, that is a defect, not a rounding question.

Also absent, not blurred: reviewer handles. A blurred handle means the build put it there - a visible or blurred handle is a build defect either way. The quotes carry neutral labels only.

It's learned from every room I've ever won.

Screen 2 - what it found

  1. Show the identity block: the show name, the host name, 885 episodes, since 2013, weekly, 4.9 from 4,575 ratings, and the volume of reviews and comments read.
  2. Pass over the six lens panels and the six verbatim audience quotes.
  3. Click "Compare to my best rooms".

Record-day trap on this screen. In the identity block, "885 episodes", "running since 2013", "updated weekly", the 4.9 ratings tile and the 41,900 YouTube tile are all real outbound links - to Apple Podcasts and to YouTube. A stray click on any of them navigates the browser clean out of the demo and kills the take. Move the cursor away from that block before you start narrating, and never park it there.

It looks at six things, the same six I used to work out by hand.

Screen 3 - the comparison (the wow)

  1. Show the appearance counts: how many rooms, how many comparable, how many landed, how many died.
  2. Hold on the losing opener line - the one that says an opener was used in three comparable rooms and died in all three. That single line is the wow moment of the whole demo. Do not talk over it.
  3. Click "Build the running order".
It compares this room to the best rooms I've ever had, and tells me what worked and what fell flat.

Screen 4 - the plan

  1. Show the four time blocks: the open, the story picks, the turn, the ask.
  2. Then show the two closing panels: what not to use in this room, and the audience's own language.
It hands me back the plan for those 45 minutes: the open, the story, the turn, the ask.
It pulls the actual words that audience uses, not my guess at their words, theirs.

On the show itself, the agreed line is:

I'm in talks with them about going on.

Say exactly that. Anything stronger claims an appearance that has not happened, and anything vaguer makes the demo look hypothetical.

The form - what to do

Name, email, and a short set of questions about the room you want to be in: what you sell, who you want in front of you, and what you want them to do afterwards. Fill it as a real owner would, all the way through, and submit it on camera.

Do not read the labels off this sheet and do not name a number of fields in the voiceover. The forms are being edited right now, so anything written down here is stale by definition. Film what is on the live page.

The mechanic - say this carefully, it is the part that gets confused

Demo 1 took a link. Demo 2 does not. The lead opens the show's page, selects all, copies, and pastes into their project. Show it happening:

  1. Open the show's page in the browser.
  2. Cmd+A, then Cmd+C, on camera. The select-all highlight has to be visible - that flash of blue is what makes the instruction land.
  3. Paste into the project.
  4. Then type this, word for word:
I'm speaking on [name of the podcast or event] in two weeks. Run the audience research.

The select-all-and-copy instruction must be visible on screen and must be inside the delivered file's sourcing prompt. If it is only in one of the two places, the lead gets stuck at the first step.

SCRIPT WORDING - your call before filming

Each item is the current line, the suggested replacement, and why it matters. You take the call - and take it before the read, not during it.

1. "All the research and legwork"

have it doing all the research and legwork for you by tomorrow
By tomorrow you can have it doing the heavy lifting - it goes and gets what it can reach on its own, and tells you exactly where to paste in the rest.

Shorter, if the beat needs to move faster:

By tomorrow it's doing the legwork. It fetches what it can, and tells you exactly what to paste in for the rest.

Why it matters: the word is all. It was tested for real on 2026-08-13 in a live project, and the honest answer is "some, and it tells you about the rest". The assistant did reach an Apple Podcasts page unaided and quoted its rating straight back - "4.9 out of 5, 4,575 Ratings" - so the old blanket claim that it cannot fetch anything is dead too. But it is blocked from Spotify and from YouTube comments, and a raw feed comes back as unreadable soup.

A viewer who hears "all" tries the second source that evening, hits a wall, and concludes either that they did it wrong or that the video oversold it. Both outcomes cost more than the sentence buys. The replacement is also the setup for Demo 2: the copy-paste step exists precisely because some sources are closed, so promising full automation two minutes earlier makes your own demo look like a workaround for a broken tool instead of the correct method.

Claim the heavy lifting. Never claim "all". "It fetches what it can and tells you where to paste the rest" is a promise the tool actually keeps.

2. The product name

Research Engine
Audience Messaging Expert

Why it matters: the deliverable has been renamed. One product name across the video, the page, the email, the file and the captions is a standing rule here, and a name that only changed in some places is worse than not changing it. Sweep your read for the old name before the take. This applies to the deliverable only. The Demo 1 screen keeps its own name, Audience Message Research, and that is an approved decision - see the note under Demo 1, Screen 1. Do not "correct" the screen.

3. Handing the viewer from link to paste

Still unwritten, and it is yours to write - Demo 1 takes a single link, and Demo 2 asks for a paste of the whole page. Both are correct in their own place. (The line "One link is all it needs" is the premise, not on-screen text - see Demo 1, Screen 1.) A starting point, not a decision:

In my system it takes a link. In yours it takes thirty seconds of copy and paste - and it does the same job.

Why it matters: without an explicit handoff the viewer assumes Demo 2 is broken, or that you switched tools between the two demos. This is item 1 in OPEN QUESTIONS below and it cannot be resolved in the edit.
