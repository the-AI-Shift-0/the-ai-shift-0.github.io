Message to Audience

Recording cheat sheet. Demo 1 = the built four-screen walkthrough. Demo 2 = the Audience Messaging Expert Master Trove opt-in.

Before you hit record

  1. Dock hidden. System Settings > Desktop & Dock > auto-hide ON.
  2. App sidebar collapsed - before the first frame, not mid-take.
  3. Bookmarks bar hidden. Cmd+Shift+B.
  4. URL bar hidden. Full screen. Where a URL has to show, show that one and nothing around it.
  5. Sign in at /demos/ first or the demo pages bounce you home mid-take.

Open tabs, bookmarks, internal URLs and folder names have all reached a customer-facing frame before.

Settled - do not reopen on the day

  • The show is named on screen, nothing blurred: The Amy Porterfield Show. Blurring was rejected.
  • Two names, both approved. Tool on screen = Audience Message Research. Deliverable (Trove, page, email, file, captions) = Audience Messaging Expert. Do not "correct" the screen.

Banned outright: any download, listener or audience-size figure. On screen, spoken, or estimated out loud. No such public number exists.

Demo 1 - the four-screen walkthrough

https://the-ai-shift-0.github.io/demos/message-to-audience/

All four screens are required - the plan at the end is the payoff.

Screen 1 - the link goes in

  • Header reads Audience Message Research.
  • The field starts empty. The link goes in on camera - have it on the clipboard.
https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-amy-porterfield-show/id594703545
It's learned from every room I've ever won.
  • Paste, then click Start Research - that is the exact label.
  • The seven-step overlay runs about 16 seconds and advances itself. Do not click through it, and do not narrate it as instant.
  • "One link is all it needs" is the premise, not on-screen text. Never say "as it says there".

Screen 2 - what it found

  • Identity block, then the six lens panels.
  • Phrases they use - six verbatim fragments, full width. The demographics column beside it was removed 2026-08-20; do not look for it.
  • No verbatim quote cards - deleted. Do not say "their own words, quoted".
  • Click Compare to your past appearances - exact label. "Compare to my best rooms" is dead wording.
It looks at six things, the same six I used to work out by hand.

Trap: in the identity block, "885 episodes", "since 2013", "weekly", the 4.9 tile and the 41,900 YouTube tile are real outbound links. One stray click leaves the demo and kills the take - park the cursor elsewhere.

Screen 3 - compared to your past appearances

  • Every number on this screen was deleted. No tiles, no counts - do not narrate one.
  • Framing line: From your own transcripts / "Read every transcript from your past appearances, then read this room. These are the insights for this opportunity."
  • Two halves: This audience does not respond to (4 bullets) and This audience typically responds to (5 bullets).
  • Click Build the running order.
It reads every transcript from the rooms I've already done, then reads this one, and tells me what this audience does not respond to and what it does.

Screen 4 - the plan

  • THREE time blocks, not four: The opening (0-5, 5 min), The body (5-34, 29 min), The end (34-45, 11 min). Total 45.
  • THREE side cards, not two: Do not use in this room / Their language, use it / Built from.
It hands me back the plan for those 45 minutes: the opening, the body, the end.
It pulls the actual words that audience uses, not my guess at their words, theirs.

On the show itself, exactly this and nothing stronger:

I'm in talks with them about going on.

Demo 2 - Audience Messaging Expert Master Trove LIVE

https://theaishift.biz/tools/message-to-audience/master-trove/
  • Page heading reads Audience Messaging Expert Master Trove. Verified live.
  • Fill the form as a real owner and submit on camera. Five fields, all required, in this order:
    • Your Name *
    • Email Address *
    • What Do You Sell? *
    • Who Do You Want in the Room? *
    • What Do You Want Them to Do Afterwards? *
  • Submit button reads Get My Audience Messaging Expert →.
  • Open the show's page, then Cmd+A, Cmd+C on camera. The blue select-all flash has to be visible.
  • Paste into the project, then type this word for word:
I'm speaking on [name of the podcast or event] in two weeks. Run the audience research.

The select-all step must be visible on screen and inside the delivered file's sourcing prompt. In one place only, the lead gets stuck at step one.

Script wording - your call before the read

1. "All the research and legwork"

have it doing all the research and legwork for you by tomorrow
By tomorrow it's doing the legwork. It fetches what it can, and tells you exactly what to paste in for the rest.

Claim the heavy lifting, never "all". It does reach Apple Podcasts unaided; it is blocked from Spotify and from YouTube comments.

2. The product name

Research Engine
Audience Messaging Expert

Sweep your read for the old name. Deliverable only - the Demo 1 screen keeps its own name.

3. Handing the viewer from link to paste

In my system it takes a link. In yours it takes thirty seconds of copy and paste - and it does the same job.

A starting point, not a decision. Without an explicit handoff the viewer assumes Demo 2 is broken, or that you switched tools between the demos.
