You Didn't Build This Business To Stay The Bottleneck
The Founders Track is an 8-week program for owners doing seven figures who are done being the only person who can close, write, or decide anything.

You know the feeling. Revenue's fine. The team's fine. But nothing important moves unless you personally push it, and you're tired of being the reason your own company is slow.

You didn't start this to spend your best hours proofreading, approving, and rewriting things other people should already be able to do without you. You started it to build something that runs - and right now it doesn't, not really, not without you standing over it.

The Founders Track exists for exactly one kind of owner: doing real revenue, running a real team, and still doing the work of three people because nobody else can be trusted to do it right.

Over eight weeks you'll rebuild how decisions actually get made in your business - not by hiring more people to manage, but by building the systems that let the people you already have make the calls you're currently making yourself.

Every week is one constraint removed. Not theory. The exact sequence we used to go from "nothing happens without me" to a business that keeps moving whether you're in the room or not.
