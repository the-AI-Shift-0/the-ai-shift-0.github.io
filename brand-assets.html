Motion Graphics Rules

✅
Always light background. Lower thirds, info boxes, stat cards - lavender-white (#f8f7ff) or white. Never dark.
✅
Purple is the only accent. Buttons, bars, borders, highlights - all #7c3aed. Never blue, teal, or orange.
✅
Flat design only. No 3D, no shadows, no gradients in UI elements. Gradient is only in the logo circle itself.
✅
Labels uppercase. Category labels and stat labels: uppercase, weight 800, letter-spaced. Body text is normal case.
✅
Channel name: Always "The AI Shift" - title case. Never all-caps in body text or lower thirds.
